All That We See or Seem

Ken Liu

Sci-Fi Thriller

Julia Z, once famous as the teenage "orphan hacker," lives in careful digital obscurity outside Boston until a lawyer begs for her help. His wife, a celebrated dream artist who guides collective dreaming experiences, has been kidnapped, and untangling the real from the unreal pulls Julia into a high-stakes chase across an AI-saturated near future. First in a new series.

Main Characters

Bobby Palmer

Literary Romance

Clara and Seb are about to fall in love. They don't know it yet, but everyone else does. Spanning two decades in London, their sweeping, messy romance is told entirely through the eyes of the people around them: friends, flatmates, rivals, and passing strangers. Everyone has a version of events, but only the main characters can decide how it ends.

Borne

Jeff VanderMeer

Sci-Fi / New Weird

In a ruined city terrorized by Mord, a giant flying bear created by a collapsed biotech company, scavenger Rachel finds a strange green lump tangled in his fur and takes it home. She names it Borne. As it grows, learns to talk, and becomes something between a child and a threat, Rachel must decide what Borne is and what loving it will cost her.

American Fantasy

Emma Straub

Contemporary Fiction

Newly divorced and stuck at a fading magazine job, fiftysomething Annie boards a nostalgia cruise headlined by Boy Talk, the boy band she adored as a teenager, after her sister bails at the last minute. Among slushy drinks and screaming middle-aged fans, she reconnects with a long-buried part of herself and unexpectedly befriends one of the band members. A funny, big-hearted story about fame, aging, and what we love.

Dead but Dreaming of Electric Sheep

Paul Tremblay

Sci-Fi Horror

Julia Flang, a former gaming streamer drifting between dead-end jobs, gets an unprecedented offer from her tech-mogul mother's company: chaperone a brain-dead man implanted with proprietary AI on a journey from California to the East Coast. A darkly funny, genre-bending nightmare about Silicon Valley's takeover of the human mind and body.

Meeting New People

Daniel M. Lavery

Literary Fiction

Barbara, twice-divorced and armed with strong opinions about everything, is blindsided when her best friend dumps her over dinner, list of grievances in hand. Looking back on the nine great friendship breakups of her life, she sets out to find her tenth and final best friend. A sharp, laugh-out-loud comedy of manners about loneliness, faith, and making friends later in life.

Dungeon Crawler Carl

Matt Dinniman

LitRPG / Sci-Fi Fantasy

Overnight, every building on Earth collapses and the survivors are invited into an eighteen-level dungeon beneath the surface, where the apocalypse doubles as an intergalactic reality show. Carl, in boxer shorts and armed mostly with spite, descends with his ex's cat, Princess Donut, who has gained sentience and a massive ego. Violent, absurd, and weirdly heartfelt.

Muñeca

Cynthia Gómez

Gothic Horror

In 1968 Oakland, Nati, a working-class witch, hears that the wealthy Miramontes family's daughter Violeta has fallen mysteriously ill. Certain it's dark magic, Nati takes a job as her caretaker, planning to break the curse and collect the reward. But as romance blooms between the two women, Nati grows reckless and must face ghosts of her own. A spellbinding gothic debut.

The Dark One

Nikki St. Crowe

Dark Fantasy Romance

Book two of the Vicious Lost Boys series, a wicked adult reimagining of Peter Pan. Winnie Darling is caught deeper in Neverland's shadows, and this time the story turns toward Vane, the Dark One himself, whose power and cruelty hide something more dangerous: what he might feel for her. Steamy, villain-loving fun.
